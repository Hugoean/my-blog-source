一句话总结:JQL(Judging Quality across Languages)用「人工标注 GT → 选 top-N LLM-as-judge → 蒸馏到 frozen 多语言 embedding + 轻量回归头」三段管线,把 Fineweb-Edu 那套英文教育价值打分扩展到 35 种欧洲语言,并对阿拉伯语/泰语/中文做 zero-shot 迁移;在用同等或更多 token 的前提下,显著超过 FineWeb2 的启发式过滤基线。

🎯 面试考点(高频):

  1. 三阶段管线(必复述):① 511 个英文文档由 15 名标注员按 Fineweb-Edu 的 0–5 教育价值 prompt 打分,再用 DeepL(22 语种)+ GPT-4o(13 语种)翻译为 35 种欧洲语言,得到 17 500 文档的多语言 GT;② 在 GT 上用 Spearman 相关系数(而非 F1/Accuracy)挑出 Gemma-3-27B-it、Mistral-3.1-24B-it、Llama-3.3-70B-it 三个 top judges,用它们给每语种 500k 个 FW2 文档各打 3 次分;③ 用 frozen Snowflake Arctic Embed v2 + 一个 MLP 回归头蒸馏,head 参数 $<1\%$,A100 单卡 $\sim 11{,}000$ docs/min(平均 690 tok/doc)。
  2. 为什么用 Spearman 而非 F1:教育价值是「有序连续语义」(0…5 是序数 ordinal,不是类别),分类指标 order-invariant、对刻度漂移敏感;而在 web 级语料里,文档的相对排名才是决定保留谁的关键。文中 Gemma-3-27B-it 排名第一但分类指标差就是反例。
  3. multilingual NLU vs NLG 解耦:全程用英文 prompt + 让模型用英文回答,让模型只动用「跨语言理解能力」,绕开低资源语种生成不稳定的问题——这是 LLM-as-judge 能跨 35 种语言保持稳定的关键 trick。
  4. 百分位阈值 vs 绝对阈值:Gemma > Mistral > Llama 给出的分数分布系统性偏移(虽 Spearman > 0.87),所以「score $\geq 3$」这种绝对阈值不可移植;JQL 改用 per-head percentile threshold(论文用 0.6 / 0.7),并要求 3 个 head 同时通过,降单模型 bias、控质量–保留量 tradeoff。
  5. 关键收益:Spanish 上 0.6 阈值比 FW2 多保留 $\sim 9\%$ tokens 还赢质量;13 语种平均:0.6 阈值 tokens $+4.8\%$ / benchmark Avg $+4.27\%$ / Final $+4.6\%$;0.7 阈值 tokens $-15.8\%$ / Avg $+6.70\%$ / Final $+7.2\%$——更严的阈值用更少 token 换更高质量。
  6. 跨语言泛化机制:Snowflake Arctic Embed v2 把跨语种语义对齐到同一嵌入空间,因此对训练时未见过的阿拉伯语/泰语/中文也能 zero-shot 直接用,无需再训(Fig.6 在 MMLU 与多语 QA 上仍优于 FW2)。三个孤立语种 Irish/Maltese/Basque 因 embed 训练语料覆盖差而表现下滑——属于 backbone 的锅,不是方法本身的锅。
  7. 消融与扩展:① 训练样本量在 500k 左右收敛;② frozen backbone + 多个 head 共享 → 同一份 embed 可以并行算教育价值/数学正确性/成人内容,边际成本极低;③ 论文消融只在 2B-LLaMA 上做了 14B/27B tokens,scaling 到更大模型未验证,是公开 limitation。
  8. 对 Shopee 的借鉴:TH/ID/PT 等低资源市场最直接可复用——拿现有 multilingual embed + 一个轻量 head + 几百条 PM 标注的「商品文案/客服回答质量」就能跑;不必每语种都重训 judge。

Abstract / 摘要p.1

High-quality multilingual training data is essential for effectively pretraining large language models (LLMs). Yet, the availability of suitable open-source multilingual datasets remains limited. Existing state-of-the-art datasets mostly rely on heuristic filtering methods, restricting both their cross-lingual transferability and scalability. Here, we introduce JQL, a systematic approach that efficiently curates diverse and high-quality multilingual data at scale while significantly reducing computational demands. JQL distills LLMs' annotation capabilities into lightweight annotators based on pretrained multilingual embeddings. These models exhibit robust multilingual and cross-lingual performance, even for languages and scripts unseen during training. Evaluated empirically across 35 languages, the resulting annotation pipeline substantially outperforms current heuristic filtering methods like Fineweb2. JQL notably enhances downstream model training quality and increases data retention rates. Our research provides practical insights and valuable resources for multilingual data curation, raising the standards of multilingual dataset development.

高质量的多语言训练数据是有效预训练大语言模型(LLM)的关键。然而,合适的开源多语言数据集依旧供给有限。现有的最先进数据集大多依赖启发式(基于规则的)过滤方法,既限制了跨语言可迁移性,也限制了规模扩展能力。在此,我们引入 JQL——一个系统化的方法,能够在显著降低算力需求的同时,大规模高效地策展多样且高质量的多语言数据。JQL 把 LLM 的标注能力蒸馏到基于预训练多语言嵌入的轻量标注器上。这些模型在多语言与跨语言场景中表现稳健,即使是训练期间从未见过的语言与文字也能胜任。我们在 35 种语言上做了实证评估,所得标注流水线显著超过 Fineweb2 这类当前的启发式过滤方法。JQL 既明显提升了下游模型的训练质量,也提高了数据保留率(retention rate)。我们的研究为多语言数据策展提供了可操作的洞见与有价值的资源,推动多语言数据集建设的标准升级。

§1 Introduction / 引言p.1
1.1 动机:数据质量决定预训练上限

The quality of pre-training data remains a crucial factor in LLM performance and represents one of the most effective factors for reducing training costs (Penedo et al., 2024a). Even recent improvements in post-training and scaling of inference-time compute heavily depend on the quality of the pre-trained base model (Guo et al., 2025). Consequently, a growing number of research efforts have focused on developing data curation pipelines for large-scale web data (Penedo et al., 2024a; Li et al., 2024; Su et al., 2024). The overall goal of any data filtering set-up is to achieve the largest possible dataset of the highest quality. Traditionally, heuristic-based approaches rely on predefined rules to filter the raw training data (Abadji et al., 2022; Penedo et al., 2024a). Recently, however, there has been a shift towards machine learning-based data curation, which tends to outperform complex rule-based systems in producing high-quality pre-training corpora.

预训练数据的质量仍然是决定 LLM 性能的关键因素之一,也是降低训练成本最有效的手段之一(Penedo 等,2024a)。即便是近期 post-training 与推理期算力扩展(inference-time scaling)的进步,也都严重依赖预训练 base model 的质量(Guo 等,2025)。因此,越来越多的研究工作开始投入到面向大规模 web 数据的策展(curation)管线中(Penedo 等,2024a;Li 等,2024;Su 等,2024)。任何数据过滤流程的总体目标都是:在质量最高的前提下拿到体量最大的数据集。传统做法依赖启发式规则(heuristic)来清洗原始训练数据(Abadji 等,2022;Penedo 等,2024a);近来则在向「机器学习驱动的数据策展」迁移,后者在构造高质量预训练语料方面通常优于复杂的规则系统。

1.2 问题:LLM-as-judge 在多语言场景下的空白

A particularly interesting research avenue is the use of existing LLMs to identify high-quality content. This "LLMs as judges to filter datasets" approach has proven highly effective in selecting high-quality data that leads to more performant models (Penedo et al., 2024a; Su et al., 2024). A significant limitation, however, is that existing research in this area largely focuses on English, making it unclear whether these methods effectively transfer to highly multilingual settings, especially those involving low-resource languages. Specifically, in contrast to English-centric data curation, multilingual settings raise additional questions on potential gaps between high- and low-resource languages and the cross-lingual performance on unseen languages. Moreover, much of the research in this field is led by frontier AI labs, which tend to keep state-of-the-art data procurement and curation strategies closed-source, impeding reproducibility and follow-up research.

其中一条特别有意思的路线是「让现有 LLM 来识别高质量内容」,即LLM-as-judge 过滤数据集。这套方法已在英文上被证明能挑出高质量数据、显著提升模型表现(Penedo 等,2024a;Su 等,2024)。但一个明显的局限是:现有研究主要聚焦英文,这些方法能否有效迁移到高度多语言的场景(尤其涉及低资源语种)还不清楚。与英文为中心的策展不同,多语言场景会额外冒出两类问题——① 高资源 vs 低资源语种之间可能存在能力鸿沟;② 模型对训练期间未见过的语言能否做到跨语言迁移。此外,这一领域的研究大多由前沿 AI lab 主导,而它们倾向于将最先进的数据获取与策展策略闭源,阻碍了可复现性与后续研究。

1.3 方法:JQL 四阶段管线

Addressing these limitations, we propose a multilingual data filtering approach called JQL (Judging Quality across Languages, pronounced "Jackal") comprising the four stages outlined in Fig. 1. With minimal human supervision and small amounts of distilled annotation data, we are able to train lightweight regressors for efficient filtering of multilingual, large-scale data at low computational cost. JQL is language agnostic and can be extended to arbitrary filter criteria. In the first stage (Sec. 2), human annotators generate ground truth (GT) annotations on monolingual documents based on an instruction set defined in a prompt; the documents are then translated into all target languages to obtain a multilingual GT dataset. In the second stage (Sec. 3), the GT dataset is used to select the top-$N$ performing LLMs-as-judges. In the third stage (Sec. 4), the resulting synthetic dataset is used to train a set of lightweight annotators at low cost by reusing shared embeddings. Using these annotators, we can efficiently annotate pre-training corpora and filter high-quality subsets (Sec. 5).

针对上述局限,我们提出多语言数据过滤方法 JQL(Judging Quality across Languages,发音同 "Jackal"),由图 1 所示的四个阶段组成:在最少人工监督外加少量蒸馏标注数据的条件下,我们能训练出轻量回归器,以极低算力代价高效过滤多语言大规模数据。JQL 与语言无关(language-agnostic),并可扩展到任意过滤准则。① 阶段一(§2):由人工标注员在单语文档上按 prompt 中定义的指令集生成 ground truth(GT),然后把这些文档翻译到所有目标语言,得到多语言 GT 数据集;② 阶段二(§3):用 GT 数据集挑出 top-$N$ 表现最好的 LLM-as-judge;③ 阶段三(§4):用上一阶段产出的合成数据集训练一组轻量标注器,通过共享 embedding 复用降低成本;④ 阶段四(§5):用这些标注器高效地为预训练语料打分,并过滤出高质量子集。

1.4 贡献与开源 artifact

Overall, we make the following contributions: (1) A human-centric approach to creating ground truth by using human annotations to build a reliable dataset for evaluating and guiding pipeline component selection. We release a novel ground truth dataset comprising 511 manually annotated documents, translated into 35 languages (Sec. 2). (2) A study investigating LLM capabilities in assessing the quality of multilingual documents (Sec. 3). We release annotations from the three best-performing LLMs across 35 languages, covering over 14 million documents. (3) A study investigating the multi- and cross-lingual transfer capabilities of lightweight annotator models, evaluating how well judgment abilities generalize to unseen languages (Sec. 4). (4) Demonstration that our approach leads to high-quality pre-training datasets that improve downstream LLM performance (Sec. 5). Table 1 lists the 35 European languages we cover, organized by family (Slavic 9, Germanic 7, Romance 7, Uralic 3, Baltic 2, plus seven singleton families: Hellenic, Celtic, Basque, West Semitic, Turkic, Albanoid, Armenian).

本文总体贡献有四点:(1)「以人为本」地构造 GT——用人工标注搭起一个可靠的基准数据集,用来评估并指导管线各组件的选型;我们开源一份新的 GT 数据集——511 个文档由人工标注,并翻译到 35 种语言(§2)。(2) 一项关于「LLM 评估多语言文档质量」能力的系统研究(§3),开源了表现最好的三个 LLM 在 35 种语言上的标注结果,覆盖超过 1400 万文档。(3) 一项关于「轻量标注器的多语言/跨语言迁移能力」的研究,评估其判断能力对未见语种的泛化(§4)。(4) 实验证明本方法产出的预训练数据集能改善下游 LLM 性能(§5)。表 1 列出覆盖的 35 种欧洲语言,按语系组织:Slavic 9、Germanic 7、Romance 7、Uralic 3、Baltic 2,外加 7 个 singleton 语系(Hellenic、Celtic、Basque、West Semitic、Turkic、Albanoid、Armenian)。

§2 Collecting Human Annotations / 收集人工标注p.2
2.1 User Study Design / 用户研究设计

To construct a multilingual ground truth dataset for selecting an LLM-as-judge for evaluating the educational value of documents, we conducted a human annotation study. As a starting point, we leveraged the English LLM-annotated dataset from Fineweb-Edu (Penedo et al., 2024a), which contains approximately 450,000 educational-value annotations. Given the demonstrated effectiveness of their scoring scheme, we adopted the same 6-point scale, ranging from $0$ (lowest educational value) to $5$ (highest). To ensure balanced representation, we sampled $100$ documents for each score level; only $11$ documents were available for score $5$, so the resulting dataset totals $511$ samples. Fifteen annotators with backgrounds in CS, English studies, physics and mathematics annotated each document three times. We employed the Fineweb-Edu educational prompt as guidelines and ran a dedicated training session—essential because, without training, some annotators partially misunderstood the task despite written guidelines. The three annotations per document were aggregated via majority voting (or averaging when no clear majority emerged).

为构造一份用于挑选「教育价值 LLM-as-judge」的多语言 GT 数据集,我们做了一项人工标注研究。起点是 Fineweb-Edu(Penedo 等,2024a)的英文 LLM 标注数据集,该集大约有 45 万条对「教育价值」的打分。鉴于其打分体系已被证明有效,我们直接沿用相同的 6 档刻度——$0$(教育价值最低)~ $5$(最高)。为了让各档分布平衡,我们对每个分数档采样 100 个文档;由于 5 分文档只有 11 个,最终数据集共 $511$ 条样本。15 名标注员(背景涵盖 CS、英语、物理、数学)各自独立标注三次。我们以 Fineweb-Edu 的教育 prompt 作为指南,并专门开了培训会——这一步至关重要,因为预试发现:即使有书面指南,没培训的标注员仍会部分误解任务。每个文档的 3 次标注,通过多数投票(无明确多数时取平均)聚合成一个分数。

2.2 Multilingual Extension / 多语言扩展

For multilingual support, we translated the English GT dataset into the 35 European languages outlined in Tab. 1. We focused on these languages because they offer a good tradeoff between linguistic diversity and well-populated language families. Nonetheless, we demonstrate in Sec. 6 that our pipeline works equally well on typologically different languages such as Chinese without any modifications. We used DeepL for the 22 languages it supports, and GPT-4o for the remaining 13 languages. To improve correctness of GPT-translated texts, we ran a language classifier over all documents and discarded those not matching the target language. Additionally, we removed prefatory phrases added by GPT-4o to ensure overall consistency.

为支持多语言,我们把英文 GT 数据集翻译到表 1 中列出的 35 种欧洲语言。聚焦欧洲语种是因为它们在「语言多样性」与「每个语系有多个代表语种」之间取得了不错的平衡。不过 §6 也展示了:对类型学上完全不同的语种(如中文),本管线无需任何改动即可直接使用。22 种语言用 DeepL 翻译,其余 13 种用 GPT-4o 翻译。为提升 GPT 翻译的正确率,我们对所有翻译结果跑了一个语种分类器,丢弃那些语种与目标不符的文档;另外我们删除了 GPT-4o 自动加的前缀短语,以保证一致性。

2.3 Inter-Annotator Agreement / 标注员一致性

To verify consistency, we analyzed the collected labels and annotator consensus. Agreement is high: a majority emerged for $78.5\%$ of documents and the overall standard deviation is $0.56$. While the annotation spread is $\leq 2$ for $86\%$ of the data, a few documents exhibited a spread $> 3$. Upon manual inspection we found the educational value of these examples is indeed highly subjective. Overall, our rigorous training and data cleaning yielded a reliable ground truth suitable for robust evaluation of ML-based annotators.

为验证标注流程是否一致,我们分析了所收集的标签与标注员共识。一致性很高:$78.5\%$ 的文档出现明确多数,整体标准差 $0.56$;$86\%$ 的数据 spread $\leq 2$,只有少数文档 spread $> 3$。人工检查发现,这些高分歧文档的教育价值本身就高度主观,所以分歧合理。整体而言,严格的培训 + 数据清洗,产生了一份可信的 GT,可用来稳健评估 ML 标注器。

2.4 Suitable Evaluation Criteria / 评估指标的选择(关键!)

Choosing an appropriate evaluation metric is essential for assessing the performance of LLM-based annotators against human GT. While standard classification metrics like F1 are appropriate for discrete categories with clear semantic boundaries (e.g., spam vs. non-spam), they are less suitable for ordered categorical labels that span a semantic continuum (e.g., very low, low, medium, high, excellent). Classification metrics are order-invariant—they fail to reflect the severity of misclassifications—and they are sensitive to scale shifts. For identifying high-quality documents in a web-scale corpus, the relative ranking of documents is significantly more relevant than adherence to an arbitrary scoring scheme. We therefore adopt Spearman correlation as our primary evaluation metric: it captures the ordinal structure of the data and is robust to monotonic scale transformations, making it well-suited for assessing models on tasks with ordered semantic categories. Key insights: well-trained human annotators can produce consistent, high-quality GT; rank-based metrics are better suited than classification metrics for model selection.

选对评估指标,对「用人工 GT 检验 LLM 标注器」至关重要。F1 这类标准分类指标只适合有明确语义边界的离散类别(如垃圾邮件 vs 正常邮件),不适合处于「语义连续谱」上的有序类别(如「很低/低/中/高/优秀」)。分类指标是order-invariant(序不变)的——它无法反映「错得多严重」,而且对刻度漂移(scale shift)很敏感。但在 web 级语料里识别高质量文档时,文档之间的相对排名远比是否符合某个绝对刻度更重要。因此我们采用 Spearman 秩相关作为主指标:它捕捉数据的有序结构,且对任何单调刻度变换都鲁棒,正契合「有序语义类别」这类任务。核心结论:① 经过良好培训的人工标注员能产出稳定、高质量的 GT;② 排名类指标比分类指标更适合 LLM 模型选型。

§3 Harnessing LLMs for Multilingual Annotation / LLM-as-judgep.3
3.1 Experimental Setup / 实验设置

We selected a diverse set of strong multilingual LLMs across sizes and families (Fig. 2): Gemma-2-9B/27B-it, Gemma-3-27B-it, Llama-3.1-8B-it, Llama-3.2-3B-it, Llama-3.3-70B-it, Mistral-3.1-24B-it, Phi-4-14B, and Qwen-2.5-7B/14B/32B/72B-it. To ensure consistency across languages and leverage the models' strong English capabilities, we used the original English FineWeb (Penedo et al., 2024a) educational prompt for all evaluations. We also instructed the models to produce English assessments, allowing us to focus on their multilingual natural-language understanding (NLU) rather than their generation capabilities (NLG). This leverages the fact that LLMs tend to have good "understanding" in low-resource languages for which they cannot reliably generate cohesive outputs (Mahfuz et al., 2025; Luukkonen et al., 2024). As in the human setup, we sampled three scores per model and aggregated them by majority/average.

我们挑选了一组多样化的强多语言 LLM 候选,横跨多个 size 与系列(图 2):Gemma-2-9B/27B-it、Gemma-3-27B-it、Llama-3.1-8B-it、Llama-3.2-3B-it、Llama-3.3-70B-it、Mistral-3.1-24B-it、Phi-4-14B,以及 Qwen-2.5-7B/14B/32B/72B-it。为保证跨语言一致性、并发挥模型最强的英文能力,我们对所有评估都统一使用原始的英文 FineWeb 教育 prompt(Penedo 等,2024a)。同时我们指示模型用英文输出评估结果,这样就只考验它的「跨语言 NLU(理解)」,而避开「跨语言 NLG(生成)」——因为 LLM 通常在低资源语种上「能读懂但生成不稳定」(Mahfuz 等,2025;Luukkonen 等,2024),这个 trick 直接利用了这一不对称性。与人工标注一样,我们对每个模型采样 3 次分,再用 majority/average 聚合。

3.2 Multilingual Evaluation / 多语言评估结果

Fig. 2 reports each LLM's Spearman correlation with the human GT. We observe substantial differences in performance both across and within model families. Notably the smallest model, Llama-3.2-3B-it, performs significantly worse than all other evaluated models (avg-35 $\approx 0.462$ vs. $0.62$–$0.70$ for larger models), suggesting that effective document quality assessment may require crossing a certain parameter threshold. Except for Llama-3.1-8B-it, all models show only limited variance across languages, supporting our hypothesis that modern LLMs exhibit robust multilingual NLU even in low-resource settings. Interestingly, Gemma-3-27B-it shows relatively poor classification performance (App. B.3) despite the strongest ranking capabilities (avg-35 $= 0.699$)—a direct illustration of why ranking metrics matter more than classification accuracy for this task.

图 2 报告每个 LLM 与人工 GT 的 Spearman 相关。我们观察到跨模型家族与同家族内部都有显著差异:最小的 Llama-3.2-3B-it 明显弱于其他所有模型(avg-35 $\approx 0.462$,而其他更大模型在 $0.62$–$0.70$),提示「有效的文档质量评估可能需要模型规模过线」。除 Llama-3.1-8B-it 外,各模型在不同语种之间的方差都很小,验证了我们的假设——现代 LLM 即使在低资源语种上也具备稳健的多语言 NLU。一个有趣的发现:Gemma-3-27B-it 的分类指标相对差(附录 B.3),但排名能力最强(avg-35 $= 0.699$),直接说明「为什么这个任务上排名指标比分类准确率更要紧」。

3.3 Top-3 Judges & Synthetic Annotations / 选出 top-3 与合成标注

Among the evaluated models, Gemma-3-27B-it, Mistral-3.1-24B-it, and Llama-3.3-70B-it emerged as the top performers from three distinct model families. We therefore used these models—referred to hereafter as Gemma, Mistral, and Llama—to generate training data for distilling annotation capabilities into lightweight annotators. Specifically, we randomly sampled up to 500k documents per language across the 35 languages from the unfiltered but deduplicated FineWeb2 (FW2) dataset, and used each model to produce three predictions per document. Key insights: strong LLMs can reliably assess educational value of web documents, and using English instructions and responses lets them judge documents even in low-resource languages. Artifact: 14M documents in 35 languages annotated by the top-3 LLMs.

在所有被评估的模型中,Gemma-3-27B-it、Mistral-3.1-24B-it、Llama-3.3-70B-it 脱颖而出——而且分别来自三个不同模型家族(避免家族 bias)。之后我们用这三个模型(下文简称 Gemma/Mistral/Llama)生成蒸馏训练数据。具体做法:从未过滤但已去重的 FineWeb2(FW2)数据集中,每种语言随机抽样最多 500k 文档,每个模型对每个文档输出 3 次预测。核心结论:① 强 LLM 能可靠评估 web 文档的教育价值;② 用英文指令 + 英文回答这一 trick,使模型能在低资源语种上做出可靠判断。开源 artifact:35 种语言、1400 万文档的 top-3 LLM 标注。

§4 Distilling Lightweight Annotators / 蒸馏轻量标注器p.4
4.1 Architecture & Backbone / 架构与 backbone 选型

We focused on cross-lingual embedding models with long context windows (Zhang et al., 2024; Sturua et al., 2024; Yu et al., 2024). Such models efficiently process long web documents and produce well-aligned representations that map semantically equivalent texts across languages to similar embeddings, enabling effective cross-lingual transfer to unseen languages. In preliminary analysis, Snowflake Arctic Embed v2.0 (Yu et al., 2024) consistently outperformed other candidates (App. C.2), and we therefore use it as our embedding backbone. Our results further indicate that keeping the embedding weights frozen while training a lightweight regression head (a simple MLP with ReLU applied to the embeddings) is sufficient to produce high-quality annotations. The final setup is highly efficient: the regression head accounts for $<1\%$ of total parameters, with embedding computation being the main runtime cost. Multiple annotators (adult-content, math accuracy, code quality, etc.) can be supported in parallel by attaching different heads to a shared backbone at minimal additional cost. Throughput: roughly $11{,}000$ annotations per minute on a single A100 with an average of $690$ tokens per document.

我们专注于支持长上下文的跨语言 embedding 模型(Zhang 等,2024;Sturua 等,2024;Yu 等,2024)。这类模型能高效处理长 web 文档,并产出「跨语种对齐」的表示——把语义等价的不同语言文本映射到相近的 embedding,从而对未见语种实现有效的跨语言迁移。预实验中,Snowflake Arctic Embed v2.0(Yu 等,2024)稳定优于其他候选(附录 C.2),因此我们选它作为 embedding backbone。结果还表明:冻住 embedding 权重、只训练一个轻量回归头(一个加 ReLU 的简单 MLP 接在 embedding 上)就足以产出高质量标注。最终设置非常高效:回归头参数只占总参数 $<1\%$,运行时主要开销在算 embedding。多个标注器(成人内容/数学正确性/代码质量等)可以共享 backbone、并行挂多个 head,几乎不增加额外成本。吞吐:在单卡 A100 上约 $11{,}000$ 标注/分钟(平均每文档 $690$ tokens)。

4.2 Multilingual & Cross-Lingual Evaluation / 多语言与跨语言泛化

Fig. 3 presents the lightweight annotators' performance. Baseline performance—training and evaluating on the same language (row 1)—is consistently strong, highlighting the robustness of our multilingual architecture. Performance only slightly decreases for checkpoints trained on all languages (last 3 rows). On average, the distilled regression heads even slightly outperform the LLMs from which their training annotations were derived; part of this gain comes from the shift to continuous labels, part reflects the strength of the pre-trained embedding model. Only three linguistically isolated languages—Irish, Maltese, and Basque—show notable degradation, likely due to limited representation in Snowflake's training data. Cross-lingual generalization is also robust: when training on languages from the same first-order family (row 2), models reliably transfer to held-out target languages; performance only slightly drops when training outside the first-order family (row 5). These results validate our motivation: aligned embedding representations enable strong cross-lingual support.

图 3 展示了轻量标注器的表现。基线(训练语种 = 评估语种,第 1 行)在所有语种上都稳定地强,说明这套多语言架构本身的鲁棒性;在「所有语种联训」(最后 3 行)的 checkpoint 上,性能只有轻微下降。平均来看,蒸馏后的回归头甚至略胜它训练时所依赖的 LLM——一部分增益来自「离散标签 → 连续标签」,另一部分则反映了预训练 embedding 自身的强度。只有三个孤立语种(Irish/Maltese/Basque)出现明显退化,原因可能是它们在 Snowflake 训练语料中覆盖不足。跨语言泛化同样稳健:用「同一一级语系内的其他语言」训练(第 2 行),模型也能可靠迁移到目标语种;甚至用「一级语系外」的语言训练(第 5 行),性能也只小幅下降。这些结果验证了我们的动机——对齐的 embedding 表示是跨语言能力的根本来源。

4.3 Building the Final Annotator / 训练最终标注器

To systematically explore the amount of data required to train the lightweight annotators, we conducted a controlled experiment across all 35 languages. Performance converged with 500k training samples (App. C.4). Building on these insights, we trained the final lightweight annotator models on a frozen Snowflake Arctic Embed v2 backbone, using 500{,}000 documents sampled evenly across all 35 languages. We trained dedicated heads for each LLM annotator—Gemma, Mistral, and Llama—to facilitate targeted comparisons and flexibility. For each annotator we considered two distinct heads: one trained on samples representative of the natural label distribution, and one trained on samples strategically selected per language to achieve the most uniform label distribution possible (heavily oversampling docs with scores 4 and 5), to counteract bias toward over-represented labels. Key insights: well-calibrated multilingual embedding models serve as powerful backbones for data annotation; lightweight regression heads enable efficient annotation and zero-shot cross-lingual transfer.

为了系统探索「训练轻量标注器到底需要多少数据」,我们在全部 35 种语言上做了控制实验:性能在 500k 样本处收敛(附录 C.4)。基于此,我们以冻结的 Snowflake Arctic Embed v2 为 backbone,在「均匀采自 35 种语言的 50 万文档」上训练最终标注器。为每个 LLM 标注源(Gemma/Mistral/Llama)分别训练专属 head,方便对照与灵活组合。对每个 head,我们考虑两种变体:① 按自然标签分布随机采样训练;② 按语种策略性采样以让标签分布尽量均匀(实际是对 4 分、5 分文档高度过采样),用来对冲「向高频标签倾斜」的偏差。核心结论:① 校准良好的多语言 embedding 是强力 backbone;② 轻量回归头能实现高效标注 + zero-shot 跨语言迁移。

§5 Assessing Training Data Quality / 评估数据过滤效果p.6
5.1 Experimental Setup / 实验设置

We conducted extensive ablation studies on the raw, unfiltered FW2 dataset (Penedo et al., 2024b). This dataset originates from Common Crawl WARC files and includes standard preprocessing (HTML extraction, language ID, dedup). Using the unfiltered raw data ensures that comparisons directly reflect differences introduced by our annotator-driven filtering, not preprocessing variations. We benchmark our annotation-based filters against FW2's original heuristic filtering. We selected $13$ representative European languages (bold in Tab. 1) and used dense decoder-only $2$B-parameter LLaMA-style models. Training data comprised $27$ billion monolingual tokens ($14$B for languages with limited data). To compare data quality across runs we used multilingual versions of MMLU (Hendrycks et al., 2021), HellaSwag (Zellers et al., 2019), and ARC (Clark et al., 2018), reporting token-normalized probability of the correct answer (smoother and more interpretable than raw accuracy at this scale). Experiments at this scale reliably predict which datasets perform better when scaling up (Magnusson et al., 2025).

我们在未经过滤的原始 FW2 数据集(Penedo 等,2024b)上做了大量消融。FW2 来自 Common Crawl 的 WARC,包含标准预处理(HTML 抽取、语种识别、去重)。用未过滤版本是为了让对照只反映过滤策略本身的差异,而非预处理差异。基线是 FW2 原生的启发式过滤。我们选了 $13$ 个有代表性的欧洲语种(表 1 中加粗),用 LLaMA 风格的 $2$B 参数 decoder-only 稠密模型;训练 token 数为 $27$ B(数据较少的语种用 $14$ B)。跨实验比较数据质量时,我们使用 MMLU(Hendrycks 等,2021)、HellaSwag(Zellers 等,2019)、ARC(Clark 等,2018)的多语言版本,并报告「正确答案的 token-normalized 概率」——在这种规模下比原始 accuracy 更平滑、更可解释。这一规模下的实验已被验证能可靠预测「scaling 到更大模型后哪份数据更好」(Magnusson 等,2025)。

5.2 Annotation Analysis / 标注分布分析 → 为什么用百分位

Fig. 4 shows a detailed statistical analysis of score distributions. First, the heuristically filtered subset of FW2 (orange) has notably higher average educational quality scores than the removed data (blue)—a sanity check confirming FW2's heuristics capture meaningful signal. Second, the regression heads trained on different LLMs exhibit significantly different absolute score distributions: Gemma-based annotators consistently assign higher scores than Mistral-based, which in turn rate higher than Llama-based. This property is inherited from the LLM annotators which use different but order-preserving scales. Despite these absolute differences, the three annotators show very high mutual agreement (Spearman's $r > 0.87$), indicating strong agreement in relative ranking. This highlights that absolute thresholds (e.g., scores $\geq 3$) lack general validity unless supported by extensive ablation. We therefore adopt percentile-based (relative) thresholds computed per regression head, enabling robust comparisons and direct control over the quality–quantity tradeoff.

图 4 对打分分布做了细致的统计分析。第一个观察:FW2 启发式过滤保留下来的子集(橙色)平均教育质量分明显高于被剔除的子集(蓝色)——这是一项 sanity check,说明 FW2 的启发式确实捕到了真实的质量信号。第二个观察:基于不同 LLM 训出的三个回归头,在绝对分数分布上显著不同——Gemma 一致地给出更高分数,Mistral 次之,Llama 最低。这种刻度差异是从 LLM 标注源继承下来的(原 LLM 之间也具有「序保持但刻度不同」的特性)。但尽管绝对刻度不同,三者两两之间的排名一致性很高(Spearman $r > 0.87$),说明它们对「谁比谁高质量」的判断高度一致。这直接意味着:「score $\geq 3$」这种绝对阈值并不通用,除非配以大量消融验证。我们因此改用 per-head percentile(每个 head 自己的百分位阈值)——既保留各自的相对排名,又能直接控制「质量 vs. 体量」的权衡。

5.3 Evaluating Pre-training Data Quality / 训练效果评估

We evaluated JQL's impact on downstream model performance by filtering pre-training data with two relative thresholds: the $0.6$ and $0.7$ percentiles per lightweight annotator head. To include a document, we required ensemble agreement across all three annotators (Gemma, Mistral, Llama), each rating the document above its respective percentile threshold. This ensemble approach reduces individual annotator bias and minimizes single-model noise. FW2's heuristic filter is our baseline. Fig. 5 demonstrates the effectiveness for Spanish; aggregated cross-lingual results are in Table 2. JQL-based filtering consistently outperforms FW2 in data quality, and stricter thresholds (0.7) consistently beat looser thresholds (0.6). Critically, JQL improves quality without excessively aggressive reduction: for Spanish, the $0.6$ threshold retains over $9\%$ more tokens than FW2 while still surpassing its quality—a property that matters especially in multilingual scenarios where many languages have limited data. Key insights: JQL outperforms heuristic filtering; percentile-based filtering is better than absolute thresholds; higher percentiles trade more tokens for higher quality.

我们评估了 JQL 对下游模型的影响:用两个相对阈值——每个轻量 head 各自的 $0.6$ 和 $0.7$ 百分位——来过滤预训练数据。纳入一个文档的条件是三 head 投票一致通过:Gemma/Mistral/Llama 都必须给它打到自己阈值之上。这种 ensemble 既降低单 head 偏差,也压制了单模型噪声。基线为 FW2 原生启发式过滤。图 5 以西班牙语为示例,跨语言聚合结果见表 2。结论很清晰:JQL 在数据质量上一致优于 FW2;阈值越严($0.7$)越好。关键的是,JQL 改善质量并不需要过激地砍数据——西班牙语上,$0.6$ 阈值比 FW2 多保留超过 $9\%$ 的 token 还能赢过它,这点在「许多语种数据有限」的多语言场景中尤其重要。核心结论:① JQL 显著优于启发式过滤;② 百分位过滤优于绝对阈值;③ 提高百分位 = 用更少 token 换更高质量。

§6 Generalization to Unseen Languages / 未见语言泛化p.7

To validate the versatile and robust cross-lingual capabilities of JQL beyond European languages, we conducted additional experiments on three linguistically and typologically distinct languages: Arabic, Thai, and Mandarin Chinese—language families completely unseen during training. We first validated the existing lightweight annotators on those languages by measuring correlation on translations of the human GT data, and observed performance similar to that on European languages (App. E.1). Consequently, we can use the existing lightweight annotators with no further training required. We applied the same dynamic percentile-based filtering (specifically the $0.7$ quantile threshold) that had proven effective on the European languages. Fig. 6 shows that even for these entirely unseen languages, the JQL pipeline maintains strong zero-shot performance, beating FW2's heuristic baseline on multilingual MMLU and on language-specific QA tasks (using benchmarks selected by FineWeb2 (Penedo et al., 2024b) since standard ones are limited for these languages). These findings confirm JQL's broad applicability and practical scalability, making it suitable for extending robust data curation into low-resource and underrepresented languages with minimal additional overhead.

为了验证 JQL 的跨语言能力超出欧洲语种之外仍然稳健,我们在三种「类型学上与训练时完全不同」的语言上做了额外实验——阿拉伯语、泰语、中文(普通话)。这三种语言所在的语系在训练期间从未见过。我们先在它们的 GT 翻译上度量了现有轻量标注器的相关系数,结果与欧洲语种相当(附录 E.1)。因此,可以无需任何再训练直接使用现成的轻量标注器。我们采用与之前相同的动态百分位过滤方法(具体用 $0.7$ 分位阈值),它在欧洲语种上已被验证有效。图 6 展示:即使对这些完全未见的语种,JQL 也保持了强劲的 zero-shot 表现,在多语言 MMLU 与「语种专属 QA 任务」(由 FineWeb2(Penedo 等,2024b)挑选,因为这些语种上标准 benchmark 较少)上均超过 FW2 启发式基线。这些发现确认了 JQL 的广泛适用性与实际可扩展性——可以以极小的额外开销,把鲁棒的数据策展能力推广到低资源与代表性不足的语种。

§7 Related Work / 相关工作 · §8 Conclusion / 结论p.8
7.1 Heuristic-Based Data Curation / 启发式过滤管线

The vast majority of LLM training data is sourced from the web, with Common Crawl (CC) the most important corpus. Traditionally, many works rely heavily—sometimes exclusively—on heuristic filtering to clean and select web data (Raffel et al., 2020; Gao et al., 2020; Weber et al., 2024; Penedo et al., 2023). These heuristics typically focus on document-level syntax (removing ill-formed or overly short texts, filtering documents containing blocklisted keywords). Web corpora are often further enriched with high-quality sources such as code, academic literature, or Wikipedia articles.

LLM 训练数据的绝大部分来自 web,Common Crawl(CC)是最重要的语料。传统上,许多工作大量(甚至完全)依赖启发式过滤来清洗/挑选 web 数据(Raffel 等,2020;Gao 等,2020;Weber 等,2024;Penedo 等,2023)。这些启发式通常聚焦在文档级语法层面——剔除格式异常或过短的文本、过滤命中黑名单关键词的文档等。web 语料常被进一步用代码、学术文献、维基百科等高质量来源补充。

7.2 Neural Data Curation / 神经网络过滤管线

A major drawback of heuristic filters is their inability to assess semantic quality. Recent work therefore incorporates neural networks into curation (Wettig et al., 2024; Su et al., 2024; Penedo et al., 2024a; Zhao et al., 2024; Li et al., 2024; Sachdeva et al., 2024; Korbak et al., 2023). To scale to billions of documents, small task-specific FastText classifiers (Joulin et al., 2016) are the most common choice. These quality annotators are increasingly trained on synthetic labels derived from strong general-purpose LLMs; in particular, annotations and filters judging educational quality have produced high-quality datasets (Su et al., 2024; Penedo et al., 2024a; Wettig et al., 2024).

启发式过滤的最大短板是评估不了语义层面的质量。近期工作因此把神经网络引入策展(Wettig 等,2024;Su 等,2024;Penedo 等,2024a;Zhao 等,2024;Li 等,2024;Sachdeva 等,2024;Korbak 等,2023)。为了 scale 到几十亿文档,最常见的选择是小型任务特化的 FastText 分类器(Joulin 等,2016)。这些质量标注器越来越多地用「强通用 LLM 生成的合成标签」来训练;尤其是基于「教育价值」的标注与过滤,已经被验证能产出高质量数据集(Su 等,2024;Penedo 等,2024a;Wettig 等,2024)。

7.3 Multilingual Data Curation / 多语言数据策展

Despite these advances, dataset curation remains largely English-centric (with a growing body of work on Chinese). Large multilingual datasets exist, but their filtering pipelines and dataset sizes are not on par with the high-quality English ones (Kudugunta et al., 2023; Nguyen et al., 2024; Brack et al., 2024; Xue et al., 2021; Burchell et al., 2025). The best-performing large-scale multilingual dataset is FineWeb2 (Penedo et al., 2024b), which relies solely on heuristic filtering. In this paper, we develop a data curation pipeline that provides advanced quality filtering in the multilingual setting and seamlessly transfers to unseen languages.

尽管已有上述进展,数据集策展整体上仍以英文为中心(中文的研究也在快速增长)。大型多语言数据集存在,但其过滤管线与数据集体量都追不上英文上的最高水平(Kudugunta 等,2023;Nguyen 等,2024;Brack 等,2024;Xue 等,2021;Burchell 等,2025)。当前性能最佳的大规模多语言数据集是 FineWeb2(Penedo 等,2024b),但它仅依赖启发式过滤。本文构建的策展管线则在多语言场景下提供了「高阶质量过滤」,并能无缝迁移到未见语种。

§8 Conclusion & Future Directions / 结论与未来方向

We proposed JQL, a multilingual pretraining data filtering approach that requires minimal human supervision and leverages language models as judges. We systematically evaluated JQL across 35 languages for filtering educationally valuable content; experiments provide extensive evidence that JQL effectively selects high-quality multilingual pretraining data, significantly outperforming heuristic-based methods. The approach is scalable to large datasets, generalizes to unseen languages, and is easily extendable. JQL opens several promising avenues for future work: (1) it is readily applicable to arbitrary filtering criteria, including code quality, mathematical correctness, and adult-content moderation; (2) it can be used not only for curating pretraining datasets but also for selecting data in various post-training stages such as instruction tuning and alignment. Our contributions lay a rigorous foundation for improved multilingual data curation and set a new standard for leveraging language and embedding models in multilingual contexts.

我们提出了 JQL——一种依赖极少人工监督、以 LLM 为裁判的多语言预训练数据过滤方法。我们在 35 种语言上系统评估了 JQL 在「过滤教育性内容」上的表现,实验充分证明:JQL 能有效挑出高质量多语言预训练数据,显著优于启发式方法。本方法可扩展到大规模数据集、可泛化到未见语种、且易于扩展。JQL 打开了多条值得探索的未来方向:① 可直接套用到任意过滤准则,包括代码质量、数学正确性、成人内容审核;② 不仅能用于预训练数据策展,还可在 instruction tuning、alignment 等 post-training 阶段挑选相关数据。本文的贡献为「改进的多语言数据策展」打下了严格的基础,并为「在多语言场景下有效利用语言模型与 embedding 模型」树立了新的标准。

Limitations / 局限p.9

Despite the breadth and generalizability of our work, we acknowledge three limitations. First, due to the infeasibility of manual annotation at scale, we machine-translated our human-annotated English ground truth into the 35 target languages rather than manually annotating ground truth in each language directly. Second, while we demonstrated JQL's effectiveness in filtering high-quality multilingual documents solely based on educational value, our approach is not limited to this criterion. JQL is designed to support arbitrary filtering objectives; we chose educational value as our primary focus because it has been shown to be a strong indicator for identifying high-quality multilingual pretraining data (Wettig et al., 2024). Third, due to high computational cost, we conducted our ablation studies at a single model scale ($2$ billion parameters). Despite this limitation, we observed consistent improvements in downstream performance, indicating the effectiveness of JQL-filtered datasets. Given the evidence of current scaling laws (Magnusson et al., 2025), we expect equally strong performance gains at larger model scales, which we leave for future work.

尽管本工作覆盖面广、可泛化性强,我们仍承认三处局限。① 翻译 vs. 原生标注:由于在每种语言上做大规模人工标注不现实,我们将人工标注的英文 GT通过机器翻译扩展到 35 种目标语言,而不是在每种语言上原生采集 GT。② 仅验证「教育价值」一个准则:我们仅基于「教育价值」展示了 JQL 对高质量多语言文档的过滤效果,但这套方法本身并不局限于此准则——JQL 设计上支持任意过滤目标;之所以选教育价值,是因为它已被证明是高质量多语言预训练数据的强指示器(Wettig 等,2024)。③ 只在单一 model scale 上做消融:由于算力开销,我们的消融实验只在 $2$B 参数这一档进行。虽然如此,下游性能仍呈现一致提升,说明 JQL 过滤数据的有效性;鉴于当前 scaling laws 的证据(Magnusson 等,2025),我们预期在更大规模上仍能拿到同样显著的提升,这部分留待未来工作。

When evaluating LLM performance, it is crucial to analyze the distribution of valid and invalid predictions to not obtain distorted conclusions. Figure 9 shows the proportion of invalid predictions across different languages. C Lightweight Annotators C.1 Experimental Setup and Parameter Choice To reduce computational overhead and accelerate development, we precomputed and cached all document embeddings prior to training. Since the embedding models remain frozen throughout training and account for over 99% of the total parameter count, this approach significantly reduces iteration time. The regression head is implemented as a lightweight neural network: a single-layer multilayer perceptron (MLP) with ReLU activation and a final linear output layer producing a scalar prediction score. We performed a hyperparameter sweep over the hidden dimension of the MLP, exploring values from 10 to 10k. Based on this search, we selected a hidden size of 1k as a robust default. Depending on the input embedding dimension, the regression head comprises approximately 770k to 1.03M trainable parameters. We trained the regression heads using the AdamW optimizer with a cosine annealing learning rate schedule, which consistently outperformed constant and linearly decaying alternatives in our experiments. The initial learning rate was set to 5 × 10−4, based on a sweep over values from 10−2 to 10−6. We also tested batch sizes from 16 to 4096 (in powers of two) and found a batch size of 1024 to offer the best balance between convergence speed and computational efficiency. We trained annotators for up to 20 epochs. To monitor generalization performance, 10% of the training data is held out for validation. We applied early stopping if the validation Spearman rank correlation fails to improve by at least 10−3 over five consecutive epochs. C.2 Backbone Selection We conducted an ablation study comparing three multilingual embedding models as potential backbones for our lightweight JQL annotators: gte-multilingual-base (Zhang et al., 2024), jina-embeddings-v3 (Sturua et al., 2024), and snowflake-arctic-embed-m-v2.0 (Yu et al., 2024). We trained a total of 18 regression heads, covering all combinations of the three embedding models and three annotation models used to generate the ground truth scores. Each combination is trained twice: once on a randomly sampled training set, and once on a class-balanced variant to mitigate the skewed distribution of education scores. Training data is sampled uniformly across all 35 languages The training setup—including hyperparameters and early stopping criteria—follows the procedure described in the previous section. Results are presented in Tab. 6. The Snowflake embedding model consistently outperforms the other backbones across annotators and training set variants. Its best configuration—combined with the Mistral3.1 annotation model and class-balanced training—yields the highest overall correlation (0.744 ± 0.016). C.4 Training Data Amount We conduct an ablation study to determine the minimum amount of training data required for our lightweight JQL annotators. To this end, we perform multiple training runs using varying amounts of data, randomly sampled from all 35 languages. The remainder of the experimental setup, including all hyperparameters, remains unchanged and is as described in C. As shown in Figure 17, using fewer than 50k training samples results in noticeably lower Spearman correlations. Performance continues to improve modestly up to approximately 500k samples. Beyond this point, adding more data does not yield significant gains, suggesting that training progress begins to converge. As expected, the number of training epochs required until early stopping decreases with larger training volumes. One advantage of using smaller training set sizes is improved class balance. Since our dataset exhibits a highly imbalanced distribution of education scores—with high and very high scores being strongly underrepresented—we do not sample randomly but instead enforce approximate class balance during data selection. Achieving this balance becomes increasingly difficult as the total number of training samples increases. Based on these considerations, we select a training set size of 500k samples. F.2 Influence of Ranking Performance and Ensembles on Data Quality In Sec. 3, we observed that Mistral achieves higher classification accuracy against human ground truth compared to Gemma, while both models exhibit similarly strong ranking capabilities. To systematically evaluate the impact of this distinction, we conducted a controlled ablation study using the Spanish subset. Specifically, we compared data filtering outcomes using single annotator models—from Gemma and Llama labels—each applying their respective 0.7 percentile thresholds independently. Additionally, this setup simultaneously allows us to assess the value of ensemble-based annotations. The results in Fig. 38 clearly indicate that the datasets filtered individually by Gemma and Llama yield very similar downstream training performance. Consequently, we can conclude that strong ranking performance is substantially more relevant than classification accuracy for the task of selecting high-quality training data. Furthermore, we observed that both single-model-filtered datasets performed worse than the dataset selected through ensemble-based annotation, thereby underscoring the robustness provided by ensemble consensus filtering. These findings emphasize the limited practical importance of absolute classification accuracy when compared to our design pipeline, which focuses on ranking capabilities and uses an ensemble to enhance annotation robustness.
